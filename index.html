What Is Movo ?

MOVO is a new generation of Digital Out-of-Home advertising. We turn moving vehicles into digital advertising platforms, putting your brand on high-brightness LED screens that travel through the streets of Cairo. Instead of waiting for people to pass your billboard, MOVO brings your message to where people already are.
